## Top Italian and French sparkling wines for New Year's Eve 2025

New Year's Eve is synonymous with celebrations, and no celebration is complete without the perfect wine. Whether you're hosting a grand party or enjoying an intimate gathering, selecting the right wine sets the tone for the evening. To help you choose, we've curated a list of the best sparkling and still wines from Italy and France that will elevate your New Year’s Eve 2025.

---

### Italian wines: Iconic and celebratory

#### **Chianti Classico Riserva - Tuscany, Italy**

- **Why choose it?** Chianti is a staple of Italian winemaking, offering a medium-bodied profile with notes of red cherry, plum, and subtle spice. Perfect for pairing with hearty dishes.
- **Perfect for:** Pairing with festive meals or gifting to Italian wine lovers.
- **Price range:** €15 – €30 per bottle.

#### **Barolo DOCG - Piedmont, Italy**

- **Why choose it?** Known as "the king of wines," [Barolo](/en/comparison/barolo-vs-nebbiolo/) is bold, structured, and elegant, with flavors of dried rose, tar, and cherry. Its impressive aging potential makes it a standout choice.
- **Perfect for:** Adding sophistication to your celebration or impressing wine aficionados.
- **Price range:** €50 – €80 per bottle.

#### **Brunello di Montalcino DOCG - Tuscany, Italy**

- **Why choose it?** Brunello offers refined complexity with flavors of dark cherry, tobacco, and leather. It’s the ideal choice for a luxurious dinner or a memorable toast.
- **Perfect for:** Special occasions or gifting to discerning wine collectors.
- **Price range:** €60 – €100 per bottle.

#### **Franciacorta Brut Contadi Castaldi - Lombardy, Italy**

- **Why choose it?** Known as Italy’s premium sparkling wine, Franciacorta is made with the traditional method, offering fine bubbles and flavors of almond and citrus.
- **Perfect for:** A sophisticated toast at midnight.
- **Price range:** €15 – €50 per bottle.

### French sparkling wines: Elegance in every sip

#### **Champagne Brut Réserve AOC Charles Heidsieck - Champagne, France**

- **Why choose it?** Blend 60/40/10, where the number indicates respectively the wines of the base year, that of the vins de réserve and the extra aging on the lees.
- **Perfect for:** Luxurious celebrations or gifting.
- **Price range:** €40 – €70 per bottle.

&nbsp;

#### **Crémant de Bourgogne Brut - Burgundy, France**

- **Why choose it?** A fantastic alternative to [Champagne](/en/academy/challenging-year-champagne-2024/), offering elegant bubbles and a floral bouquet at a fraction of the price.
- **Perfect for:** Budget-friendly yet stylish celebrations.
- **Price range:** €15 – €30 per bottle.

#### **Blanquette de Limoux - Languedoc, France**

- **Why choose it?** One of France’s oldest sparkling wines, with a refreshing profile of white flowers and stone fruits.
- **Perfect for:** Traditional yet charming toasts.
- **Price range:** €12 – €25 per bottle.

### Price range comparison: High-end, mid-range, and low-end sparkling wines

Choosing the perfect wine often comes down to your budget and the type of celebration you’re planning. Here's a breakdown of what to expect across different price ranges:

#### High-end wines (€50 and above)

- **Examples:** Vintage Champagne, Barolo, [Brunello](/en/standing/10-best-tuscan-reds/).
- **What to expect:** Exceptional quality, complex flavors, and a luxurious experience. High-end wines are often aged longer and come from top-tier producers.
- **Perfect for:** Gifting, formal parties, and special toasts.

#### Mid-range wines (€20 – €50)

- **Examples:** Franciacorta, Chianti Classico Riserva, non-vintage Champagne.
- **What to expect:** A balance of quality and affordability. These wines deliver refined flavors and are versatile for both casual and formal celebrations.
- **Perfect for:** Toasting with friends or as a thoughtful yet affordable gift.

#### Low-end wines (under €20)

- **Examples:** Prosecco, Crémant, affordable Chianti.
- **What to expect:** Lighter flavors, approachable profiles, and great value for money. Low-end options are ideal for large gatherings where quantity is key.
- **Perfect for:** Casual parties, aperitifs, or complementing festive snacks.

---

### How to pair wines for New Year's Eve

- **Appetizers:** Pair Prosecco or Crémant with light bites like smoked salmon or bruschetta.
- **Main courses:** Barolo and Brunello complement hearty dishes such as beef tenderloin or truffle pasta.
- **Desserts:** Chianti works wonderfully with dark chocolate desserts, while a sweet sparkling Moscato pairs perfectly with fruit tarts.
