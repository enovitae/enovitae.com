Editorial team

The Enovitae editorial team. The name joins «eno», Greek for wine, and «vitae», Latin for life: wine written about as an experience rather than a set of facts. We cover grapes and regions, compare the denominations people most often confuse, and taste bottles in Italy and beyond, aiming to explain what is actually in the glass without unnecessary jargon.
